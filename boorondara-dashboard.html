How to read every number here
Measured

Recorded on a garden by assessment or field note. Sits in the Registry ledger and audits back to a specific property.

Derived

Arithmetic on measured records — counts, averages, distributions. No assumptions added.

Modelled

A projection built on stated assumptions. Each assumption is printed beside the figure and adjustable in the scaling model.

Corridor effect

Connectivity and latent demand

A garden's ecological value is not independent of its neighbours. The connectivity pillar scores proximity to parks, adjacent registered gardens, and corridor nodes — so two registered gardens near each other are worth more than two far apart. Overlaid here is the demand layer: residential addresses that have looked up their EVC through the discovery tools but have not yet registered.

Why the numbers hold up

Methodology and integrity
  • One canonical scoring engine runs every score — public site, self-enrolment form and assessor tool share the same logic, with a parity test suite blocking any change that would let the implementations diverge.
  • Derived fields regenerate from input files on every sync, so a score cannot drift from the evidence that produced it. No figure is hand-adjusted to reach a target.
  • Scoring is being aligned to Accounting for Nature's Econd framework, benchmarked against Victoria's Ecological Vegetation Classes.
  • Self-reported gardens are visually quarantined and excluded from every verified total and average on this page. Only a paid, on-site Ecological Garden Health Check moves a garden to verified.
  • Growth does not depend on Gardener & Son's installation capacity — gardens are enrolling from outside the studio's client base, including the first non-G&S install at Canterbury.
  • Every figure carries its provenance tag. Modelled figures are labelled, their assumptions printed, and their inputs adjustable.
