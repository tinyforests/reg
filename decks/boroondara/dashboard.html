Privacy by design

Precise property locations are used to calculate ecological context and network relationships but are not published. Council views show de-identified records at a 130 m display grid — no names, no house numbers, no coordinate export. Lookup intensity is aggregated by suburb and suppressed below 5 records per area. Steward-consented detail is a separate, explicitly granted view.

06 · Evidence, demand and change

How reliable the evidence is, who is already asking, and what is moving

Verification status is recorded separately from score and never aggregated as equivalent. Provisional records are counted but excluded from condition reporting.

Evidence provenance

A record's score and its verification status are two different fields.

Evidence coverage

Assessment is currently carried out by the Registry's own assessors. Independent or co-verification is a known methodology development, not yet in place, and is disclosed here rather than implied.
