You already count private gardens as environmental infrastructure.

of Melbourne's tree canopy cover sits on private land.

02 · The asymmetry

The building has a rating. The living landscape has no common ecological record.

The building
The land around it
Rated before it is built. New Victorian homes have had to reach 7 stars under NatHERS since 1 May 2024.
No common measure of ecological condition, before or after.
One unit that buyers, builders and government all read the same way.
Canopy and native vegetation controls apply on some sites. Soil, understorey, habitat and species carry no dated record.
A method any accredited assessor can repeat.
No shared method a second person can apply and get the same answer.
The rating stays with the dwelling.
What was there is lost when the block is cleared or the owner changes.
Source: Victorian Government, energy efficiency standards for new homes (NCC 2022, in effect 1 May 2024)

03 · One block, four moments

No before, so no way to see what goes back.

A dated baseline before works, an as-built record after, and a condition update over time. That is all it takes to make loss and recovery visible on the same title.

04 · What the Registry records

A property-level record, scored on five pillars.

A dated baselineWhat the place held before anything changed.
Change over timeEach update is stamped with its date, source and method version.
Its place in the networkNearest reserve, neighbouring records, the cluster it belongs to.
Method v1.0 is published and versioned. Evidence source and verification status are shown on every record.

05 · Why score at all

One score, two jobs.

For institutions

A rating people can read

0 to 100, six named tiers. A consistent scoring method; independent assessor repeatability still needs testing.

For stewards

A reason to keep going

badges earned across gardens, each for a feature that exists on the ground. gardens sit within 5 points of their next tier.

The inputs are the truth. The score is the consequence. · Ledger read

10 · What we have not solved

Where this is not yet good enough.

01
Independent verification of records were verified by our own studio and by site visit. None has been verified by a third party yet.
02
Benchmarking against the reference communityVersion 1 uses fixed thresholds. A second method that scores a place against what belongs there is in build and is not quoted until calibrated.
03
Scale beyond one studio of records were enrolled by us. The ledger has to grow through stewards, other designers and council programs.
04
Privacy by designPrecise locations are held privately and public pins are offset about 250 m. No resident data moves from a council to the Registry.

11 · The wider fabric

One living system, four kinds of ownership.

Private gardens

Where we began

The largest share of urban land with the thinnest record. Most of the ledger sits here.

Apartment common land

First record held

A Surrey Hills block recorded before any intervention, so its baseline exists.

Institutional and corporate

Not yet

Campuses, schools, hospitals and business parks. The method could be adapted, subject to testing.

Public green space

In preparation

Recording public gardens beside the private ones around them, so the pattern reads as one.
