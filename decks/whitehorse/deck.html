06 · Why Whitehorse

Where we think this could be useful.

01 A clearer picture of private land Where the good gardens are, where the habitat gaps are, and where clusters are beginning to form.
02 Connectivity beyond council land A reserve may sit isolated — but twenty nearby gardens can become stepping stones between larger habitat areas.
03 Recognising residents already doing the work A way to say we see what you're already contributing, rather than only what you should be doing.
04 Evidence over time Whether gardens are actually developing better structure, habitat, soil and water function, and connectivity.
05 A framework that persists Programs come and go. The record of a garden and its contribution can carry on across years, and across owners.
