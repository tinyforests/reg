How attribution works

Provenance schema
  • Three actors are recorded separately against every garden: the designer who made it, the steward who holds it, and the verifier who assessed it. None can overwrite another.
  • Designer attribution is permanent and survives sale of the property. A garden changes hands; the record of who designed it does not.
  • The steward controls what is public — address is always private; species list, score and story are shown by default. A designer sees aggregate performance across their attributed gardens regardless.
  • Enrolment is free and does not require the designer's involvement. A steward can register a garden and name its designer; the designer is notified and can claim the attribution.
  • Verification is independent of design. A garden designed by one practice may be verified by another party — that separation is what makes the score citable.
  • Nothing about attribution changes the score. Design provenance is a record, not a scoring input.
A designer's real portfolio is not the photographs. It is what the gardens are still doing twenty years later — and until now there has been no way to show it.The proposition

Current status

Not yet built

The provenance schema — designer attribution field and a designers registry with stable IDs — is identified as required infrastructure and currently deferred. This portal is a concept showing what it would enable, built to open a conversation about whether it is worth building.

Built as a conversation piece, not a product announcement. Everything here is open to being wrong.
