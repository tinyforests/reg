A SHARED METHOD

From planting plan to lasting record.

Submit the garden’s measured inputs, indigenous species list and supporting evidence through your private practice portal. Follow its review and see your published, attributed gardens together.

The Registry uses the same five pillars for every garden: biodiversity, soil and water, habitat, connectivity, and evidence. Design attribution is recorded separately from verification.

Start with one garden.

01 · INVITATION

Open your practice portal.

Gardener & Son provides a private access link. It shows your practice’s records and submissions.

02 · SUBMISSION

Document what is there.

With steward permission, add known measurements, a species list and an evidence link. Unassessed inputs can remain unknown.

03 · REVIEW

Build a public record.

Submissions stay private during review. A published garden receives a profile; verification requires supporting evidence and an explicit reviewer.

Stewards stay in control.

The garden’s street address and steward email are held privately. Public profiles show the garden’s agreed name, suburb and ecological record. Practice access can be revoked or replaced by the Registry.

This first pilot supports garden submissions, review and an attributed portfolio. Yield payments are not active.
