# Brand sponsorship and Yield

**13 Oct 2026 · Public enquiry entry; programme activation pending.**

`brands.html` introduces proposed sponsorship of ecological gardens, stepping
stones and corridors. Homepage links appear on desktop, mobile and the footer.
The enquiry prepares a reviewed email draft locally. It stores nothing, sends no
message automatically and accepts no payment. There are no sponsor accounts,
signed partners, fixed sponsorship prices or active steward payouts implied.

## Ecological and brand value

Support may sustain indigenous planting, habitat, soil/water care and long-term
stewardship. A corridor or stepping-stone designation requires ecological evidence,
not proximity alone. Current records provide context, not a list of gardens
available to buy or sponsor. Participation and selection require steward agreement.

Proposed brand benefits are a locality-specific contribution, documented funded
work and follow-up, suitable staff learning, consented stories and multi-season
support. Reporting needs an agreed scope; it does not establish company-wide
nature-positive status, an offset, a credit, verified cooling, water savings or
financial/marketing returns without separate evidence.

Public claims should identify the contribution, time period, evidence and limits.
Use the [ACCC environmental-claims guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/about-us/publications/a-guide-to-making-environmental-claims-for-business).
Ecological context is supported by [City of Melbourne's urban forest overview](https://participate.melbourne.vic.gov.au/urban-forest/project-overview),
not used as proof of outcomes at any individual Registry garden.

## Before a partnership begins

Agree participating sites and consent; costed scope; funding allocation between
care, work, assessment/reporting and administration; payment timing and recipients;
conflict/verification arrangements; dated baseline and follow-up; shortfall handling;
and permitted sponsor acknowledgement. Distinguish maintenance of existing value
from newly enabled improvement and avoid double-counting multiple funders.

Street addresses, contact details and access/visit arrangements remain private.
No brand gets a participant list or access to a garden by sponsoring it. Stories,
photos, visits and acknowledgement require appropriate participant permission.
Council consent and commercial-separation commitments remain unchanged.

## Activation boundary

Yield remains sponsor-funded stewardship support in development. Canonical garden
yield values, the current scoring engines and hidden profile rendering are unchanged.
An agreed sponsorship scope does not by itself activate annual payouts or the
placeholder formula. Follow `revenue-and-yield-rules.md` before changing any live
status, amount or forecast. Log the actual agreement and activation when they occur.

The public page is an enquiry and proposal surface; budgeting, disbursement,
contracting, reporting and a sponsor portal remain future implementation work.
