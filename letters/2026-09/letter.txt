WHAT TO PLANT THIS MONTH

September into October is the planting window in Melbourne: soil still
holding winter moisture, days warming, and roots have months to settle
before summer bites. Plant grasses and groundcovers first — they knit
the ground and shelter everything that comes after.

A GARDEN ON THE RECORD

On Sir Garnet Road in Surrey Hills, Richard and Jenny's front garden
carries a small sign on its fence. Behind it is a narrow buffer of
species that belong to that ground, and a public record of what's
growing there — scored on biodiversity, habitat, soil and water,
connection, and evidence.

The sign does something the planting alone can't: neighbours stop, read
it, and ask. Several of the gardens now on the Registry started as
exactly that conversation.

Read the garden's record:
https://ecologicalregistry.org/profile.html?g=sirgarnet30&src=fmeg-sep26


PUT YOUR GARDEN ON THE RECORD

The Ecological Registry is a public record of gardens doing real
ecological work. You enrol your own garden through a short online form —
a few questions about what you've planted, your address, and a photo.
Ten minutes, give or take. You don't need a finished garden: a few
plants that belong and the intention to add more is enough. The record
grows with it.

The first ten gardens to enrol before 30 September get a Registry sign
for their front fence, free.

Enrol your garden:
https://ecologicalregistry.org/enrol/?src=fmeg-sep26

Your precise address is never shown publicly.
