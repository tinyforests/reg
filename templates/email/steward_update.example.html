Recorded nearby · 18 October
A Bogong Moth, about 3km from Bush Garden
Bogong Moths fly each spring from inland Queensland, New South Wales and western Victoria to the Australian Alps, and can turn up around Melbourne on the way.
The Correas and eucalypts growing at Bush Garden are among the native plants Bogong Moths feed on.
